# Frequently asked questions

## Why can't I change anything?

Either your role is **Viewer** or the calendar is archived and therefore read-only. See [Roles in the club](help:getting-started/roles) for what each role may do; admins can change a role ([Members](help:team/members)).

## Why don't I see "Publish"?

Only editors and admins see feeds. As a viewer you lack the role, see [Roles in the club](help:getting-started/roles).

## What is the difference between draft and published?

A draft is visible to club members only; a published event also appears in feeds. New events start as drafts, see [Your first event](help:events/first-event).

## Who can see my events?

Club members see all events. Visitors only see published events, and only through a feed you share with them, see [The public view](help:publishing/public-view).

## What is the difference between a calendar and a feed?

A calendar holds your club's events. A feed publishes one or more calendars with a link and a subscription address, see [What is a feed?](help:publishing/feeds).

## Can I get a deleted event back?

No, deleting cannot be undone. Only an event you just moved can be restored with **Undo**, see [Edit events](help:events/edit). To hide an event, set it to **Draft** instead.

## How do I invite someone to the club?

Admins send an invitation by e-mail; the person accepts it themselves, see [Invitations](help:team/invitations).

## I received an invitation but have no access. What now?

Sign in with the e-mail address the invitation was sent to and confirm it. If that does not help, see [Solving problems](help:support/troubleshooting#invitation-invalid-or-expired).

## How do I create many events at once?

For regular events use an [event series](help:organize/series), for existing lists use [import](help:organize/import).

## How do I get events into my calendar app?

Use a feed's subscription address or a file export, see [Subscribe to a calendar](help:publishing/subscribe) and [Export](help:organize/export).

## Can I use several clubs or calendars?

Yes, you can be a member of several clubs, and a club can have several calendars, see [Calendars](help:settings/calendars) and [Club](help:settings/club).

## How do I change my e-mail address or password?

Both are in your profile, see [Change e-mail address](help:settings/email-change) and [Password](help:settings/password).

## I forgot my password.

Use the reset function on the sign-in page; you get a link by e-mail (valid for 60 minutes). Other ways to sign in: [Passkeys](help:settings/passkeys).

## Does the app see my password?

No. The password is checked on your device and never leaves it, see [Password](help:settings/password#how-your-password-is-protected). An older account is converted automatically the next time you sign in.

## How do I connect an AI assistant?

It takes a few steps, see [Connect an AI assistant](help:ai-assistants/connect).

## How do I delete my account?

See [Delete account](help:settings/delete-account). If you are the last admin of a club, delete the club or hand over the role first.
