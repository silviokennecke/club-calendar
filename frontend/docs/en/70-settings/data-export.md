# Download your data

You can download a file with all the data stored about you: your account, your memberships, your sign-in history, your consents and the content you created or last edited in clubs.

## How to

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile) and the **Privacy** tab.
2. Click **Download data**.
3. Confirm that it is you, see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation).
4. The file is saved in your downloads folder. You can open it with any text editor.

You also get an e-mail telling you that your data was downloaded.

## How often

You can download the file once per hour at most. If an attempt fails, it does not count.

## What is not included

- Your password and other secrets: for security they are not stored in a readable form.
- Data of other people, for example member lists: it does not belong to you.

## If it does not work

If the app says the download is not possible right now, usually your e-mail address is not confirmed yet or the current legal documents are not accepted. Take care of that first (see [Legal and consent](help:settings/consent)). If it still does not work, ask the operator of the site.
