# Personal settings

These settings only apply to you. You find them in your [profile](help:settings/profile) under **Personal settings**. Changes apply immediately and are saved.

## Language

Under **Language** you choose German or English.

## Appearance

Under **Appearance** you choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as system**. With **Same as system** the app follows your device. This setting only applies in this browser.

## Date format and first day of the week

**Date format** sets how dates are shown. With **First day of the week** you choose **Monday** or **Sunday**, which decides where a week starts in the calendar.
