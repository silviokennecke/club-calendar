# Passkeys

With a passkey you sign in by fingerprint, face recognition or device PIN, without a password.

You manage passkeys in the section **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** on your [profile](help:settings/profile), tab **Security**. More under [Multi-factor authentication (MFA)](help:settings/mfa).

## Set up a passkey

1. In the section, click **Add MFA method** and choose **Passkey**. Click **Next**.
2. If asked, confirm that it is you, see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation).
3. Click **Next** and confirm on the device.
4. Optionally enter a name for the passkey, for example "Office PC", and click **Save name**. It helps you recognize the device in the list.

You set up one passkey per device. If your browser does not support passkeys, you see a notice.

## Sign in with a passkey

On the sign-in page, click **Sign in with passkey** and confirm on the device.

You get an e-mail when a passkey is added or removed. If you did not do this yourself, change your password right away.

## Rename a passkey

Next to the passkey, click **Rename**, change the name and click **Save**.

## Remove a passkey

Next to the passkey, click **Remove**, confirm it is you if asked (see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation)) and click **Remove passkey**. You can no longer sign in with that device by passkey.

:::warning
When you remove a passkey, your other devices are signed out.
:::

:::note
You cannot remove your last passkey while MFA is on and no authenticator app is set up. More under [Multi-factor authentication (MFA)](help:settings/mfa).
:::
