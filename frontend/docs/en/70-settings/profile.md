# Profile and name

Your profile is the page for everything about your account.

## Open your profile

Click your name at the very bottom of the sidebar (**My profile**). On a phone, use **More**. At the top of the page you see your name and e-mail address.

## Change your name

Under **Name and email address** you set how you appear to the other members of your clubs.

1. Edit the **Name** field.
2. Click **Save name**.

The name must not be empty and can be at most 255 characters.

## What else is on the page

All other account settings are listed one below the other, see the [overview](help:settings/index). **Sign out** signs you out on this device.
