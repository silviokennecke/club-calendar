# Profile and name

Your profile is the page for everything about your account.

## Open your profile

Click your name at the very bottom of the sidebar (**My profile**). On a phone, use **More**. The profile has four tabs: **Account**, **Security**, **Calendar & apps** and **Privacy**.

## Change your name

In the **Account** tab, under **Name and email address** you set how you appear to the other members of your clubs.

1. Edit the **Name** field.
2. Click **Save name**.

The name must not be empty and can be at most 255 characters.

## Renew your calendar link

If you subscribed to a club calendar through [My club](help:getting-started/my-club), the section **My calendar subscription** in the **Calendar & apps** tab lists your clubs. Click **Renew link** next to a club and confirm. The old link stops working, and you enter the new link in every calendar app where you use it. Do this if you passed the link on by mistake.

## What else is on the page

All other account settings are spread over the four tabs, see the [overview](help:settings/index). **Sign out** signs you out on this device. For sensitive actions the app asks you to [confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation).
