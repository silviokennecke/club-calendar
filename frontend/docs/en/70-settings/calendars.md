# Managing calendars

A club can have several calendars, for example one per season or department. This is where you create and tidy them.

:::note
Only **Admins** can create, duplicate, archive and delete calendars. Everyone else can open or view them.
:::

Open **Manage club** in the sidebar and switch to the **Calendars** tab.

![Calendars tab with active and archived calendars](shot:calendars-list)

## Create a calendar

1. Click **Create calendar**.
2. Enter the **Calendar name**, for example "Shooting season 2027".
3. Click **Create calendar**.

A club can have up to 100 calendars (archived ones count). You change the time zone and more afterwards in the [calendar settings](help:settings/calendar-settings).

## Duplicate a calendar

**Duplicate** creates a copy named "Copy of …". This is handy for the next season.

- Badges, extras and templates are copied.
- Locations and audiences belong to the club and are available in every calendar anyway.

## Archive a calendar

**Archive** makes a calendar read-only: nobody can create, change or delete events. It stays in the **Archived calendars** list and you can open it with **View**.

:::warning
The feeds of an archived calendar deliver no events while it is archived. If all calendars of a feed are archived, its public page is no longer available.
:::

**Restore** makes the calendar active again, and the feeds show its events again.

## Delete a calendar

1. Click **Delete** on the calendar (also possible for archived calendars).
2. Read in the dialog what will be lost. It names the number of events.
3. Confirm with **Delete calendar**.

:::warning
Deleting is final. The calendar and all its events are gone. Feeds that contain only this calendar are deleted too, and their addresses stop working. Other feeds only lose this calendar.
:::

If you just want the events out of sight, archive the calendar instead.
