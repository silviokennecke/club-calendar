# Change your e-mail address

You sign in with your e-mail address, and notifications are sent to it.

:::note
You need a password for this. If you only sign in through a provider, set a [password](help:settings/password) first.
:::

## Steps

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile), section **Name and email address**.
2. Enter the **New email address** and your **Current password**. The password is checked on your device and never sent to the server.
3. Click **Send confirmation link**.
4. Open the link in the e-mail sent to the **new** address. It is valid for 24 hours.

Your address only changes after you click the link. Until then the profile shows **New address waiting for confirmation**, where you can cancel with **Discard change**.

## What happens afterwards

- You are signed out everywhere and sign in again with the new address.
- A notice goes to the old address so nobody can change your address unnoticed. A notice also goes to the current address as soon as you request the change.

:::tip
If no e-mail arrives, the new address may already belong to another account.
:::
