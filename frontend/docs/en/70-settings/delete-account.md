# Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time. This cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile) and go to the **Danger zone**.
2. Click **Delete account**.
3. Confirm:
   - With a password: enter it under **Your password to confirm**. It is checked on your device and never sent to the server, see [Password](help:settings/password#how-your-password-is-protected).
   - Without a password (provider or passkey only): click **Send code**. You get a 6-digit code by e-mail and enter it under **Confirmation code**. It is valid for at least 10 minutes.
4. Click **Delete account**.

## What is deleted

Your account, your passkeys and linked sign-ins. You are signed out.

## When it is not possible

:::warning
While you are the only member of a club, you cannot delete your account. Delete the club first or invite someone.
:::

:::warning
If you are the only admin of a club that has other members, make someone else an admin first or delete the club.
:::

Too many failed attempts block the confirmation briefly. Wait a while and try again.
