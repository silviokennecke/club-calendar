# Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time. This cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile), tab **Privacy**, and go to the **Danger zone**.
2. Click **Delete account**.
3. Confirm that it is you, see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation). This also works when the app otherwise only shows the consent or e-mail confirmation screen.
4. Click **Delete account**.

## What is deleted

Your account, your passkeys and linked sign-ins. You are signed out.

## When it is not possible

:::warning
While you are the only member of a club, you cannot delete your account. Delete the club first or invite someone.
:::

:::warning
If you are the only admin of a club that has other members, make someone else an admin first or delete the club.
:::
