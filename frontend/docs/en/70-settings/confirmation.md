# Confirm it is you

Some actions change how you sign in or delete your account. Before the first one, the app asks you to confirm it is you. This happens once, not for every action.

## What you need

- Your password. If your account has no password, click **Send code** and enter the code from the e-mail instead.
- If multi-factor authentication (MFA) is on: also a code from your authenticator app, a backup code, or your passkey.

## How long it lasts

The confirmation is valid for 10 minutes. In that time the app does not ask again for these actions. After that, or after you sign out or change your password, it asks again. It applies only to the browser tab you used.

## Which actions

- [Change your password](help:settings/password)
- [Change your e-mail address](help:settings/email-change)
- [Delete your account](help:settings/delete-account)
- Turn MFA on or off, set up the authenticator app, create new backup codes (see [Multi-factor authentication (MFA)](help:settings/mfa))
- [Add or remove a passkey](help:settings/passkeys)
- [Link or unlink a sign-in provider](help:settings/linked-accounts)

:::note
If you use a backup code to confirm, it is used up.
:::

Too many wrong codes block the confirmation for a while. Wait and try again.
