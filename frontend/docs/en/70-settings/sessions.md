# Sessions and signing out

A session lasts until you sign out, but at most 30 days after signing in. You also stay signed in when you open a new tab or close and reopen the browser.

## Sign out on this device

Click **Sign out** in the sidebar or on your [profile](help:settings/profile). Then you are signed out in all open tabs of this browser.

Tip: on someone else's or a shared computer, always sign out when you are done. Otherwise you stay signed in even after closing the browser.

## Sign out everywhere

Useful if you lost a device or stayed signed in on someone else's device.

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile), tab **Security**, and go to **Sign-ins**.
2. Click **Sign out everywhere** and confirm.

All active sessions end, including this one. Connected AI apps lose access too.

## Sign out other devices

There is no list of individual devices. Instead, change your [password](help:settings/password): you stay signed in on this device, all others are signed out. Resetting your password, changing your e-mail address or removing a passkey also ends sessions.
