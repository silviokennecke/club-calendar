# Linked sign-ins

If you link your Google or Microsoft account, you can use it to sign in without a password.

## Link an account

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile) and go to **Linked sign-ins**.
2. Click **Link account** next to the provider. If asked, confirm that it is you, see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation).
3. Sign in with the provider and confirm. You return to your profile, which now shows **Connected**.

If multi-factor authentication (MFA) is on for your account, an address that is not linked yet cannot sign in with Google or Apple. Sign in with your password and MFA first, then link the provider here. Providers you already linked keep working. You get an e-mail when you link one.

If the confirmation ran out while you were at the provider, the page asks again. Confirm, and the link completes (within 5 minutes).

A provider account can be linked to only one Club Calendar account, and one account per provider is possible.

## Unlink

Click **Unlink** next to the provider and confirm. If asked, confirm that it is you (see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation)). You can no longer sign in with it. Your other ways to sign in stay.

:::warning
At least one sign-in method must remain. Set up a [password](help:settings/password) or a [passkey](help:settings/passkeys) first before you unlink the last one.
:::
