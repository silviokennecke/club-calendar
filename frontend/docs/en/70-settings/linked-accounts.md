# Linked sign-ins

If you link your Google or Microsoft account, you can use it to sign in without a password.

## Link an account

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile) and go to **Linked sign-ins**.
2. Click **Link account** next to the provider.
3. Sign in with the provider and confirm. You return to your profile, which now shows **Connected**.

A provider account can be linked to only one Club Calendar account, and one account per provider is possible.

## Unlink

Click **Unlink** next to the provider and confirm. You can no longer sign in with it. Your other ways to sign in stay.

:::warning
At least one sign-in method must remain. Set up a [password](help:settings/password) or a [passkey](help:settings/passkeys) first before you unlink the last one.
:::
