# Legal and consent

To use this app you agree to the privacy policy and the terms of use. You tick the boxes when you sign up.

## View documents and history

In your [profile](help:settings/profile), tab **Privacy**, under **Legal & consents** you find the **Current documents** (imprint, privacy policy, terms of use) and the **Consent history**: which version you agreed to and when. **View** lets you read a version. **Current** or **Outdated** shows whether it is the latest.

## When documents change

- While you are signed in, a notice **Legal documents have changed** appears. **Review and accept** lets you read and accept the new version, **Later** closes it.
- At your next sign-in at the latest you are asked. The **Consent required** page appears, and only after **Accept and continue** can you keep using this app.

On that page you can **Sign out** instead or delete your account (see [Delete your account](help:settings/delete-account)).
