# Password

You sign in with your password. It must be between 8 and 128 characters long.

## Change your password

1. Open your [profile](help:settings/profile), tab **Security**, and go to **Password**.
2. Enter the **New password** twice.
3. Click **Change password**. If asked, confirm that it is you, see [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation).

You stay signed in on this device. On all other devices you are signed out.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, at the password step, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your account's e-mail address and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the e-mail. It is valid for 60 minutes and works only once.
4. Choose a new password and click **Save password**.

Afterwards you are signed out everywhere and sign in again with the new password. If no e-mail arrives, check your spam folder.

## No password yet?

If you sign in only with Google, Microsoft or a passkey, your account has no password. In your profile, tab **Security**, under **Password**, click **Set password by email**. You get a link (valid for 60 minutes) to choose a password.

## Confirm it is you

For sensitive actions such as this one, the app asks you once to confirm it is you. See [Confirm it is you](help:settings/confirmation).

## E-mails about changes

When your password changes, you get an e-mail. The same applies when you turn multi-factor authentication (MFA) on or off, create or use backup codes, add or remove a passkey, or link a sign-in provider. If sign-in fails repeatedly, you also get an e-mail. If you did not do this yourself, change your password right away.
