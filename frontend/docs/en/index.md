# Welcome

Here you learn step by step how to use the club calendar. New here? Start with [Creating an account](help:getting-started/sign-up).

## I want to …

- **get started**: [create an account](help:getting-started/sign-up), [set up a club and calendar](help:getting-started/set-up) and [get to know the app](help:getting-started/tour).
- **add and find events**: [Your first event](help:events/first-event), [Editing events](help:events/edit), [Views](help:events/views) and [Search and filter](help:events/search-filter).
- **save work**: with [templates](help:organize/templates), [event series](help:organize/series), [import](help:organize/import) and [bulk actions](help:organize/bulk).
- **manage locations, properties and target groups**: see [Organize](help:organize/index).
- **publish or subscribe to the calendar**: see [Publishing](help:publishing/index) and [Subscribe to a calendar](help:publishing/subscribe).
- **invite members**: see [Team](help:team/index) and [Invitations](help:team/invitations).
- **connect an AI assistant**: see [AI assistants](help:ai-assistants/index).
- **manage my account or my club**: see [Settings](help:settings/index).
- **get help with a problem**: see [Help and support](help:support/index), for example [FAQ](help:support/faq) and [Troubleshooting](help:support/troubleshooting).
