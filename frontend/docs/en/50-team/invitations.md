# Invitations

Nobody is simply added to a club. Anyone who should join receives an invitation and accepts it themselves.

## Invite someone

:::note
You need the Admin role for this.
:::

1. Open **Manage club** and the **Members** tab.
2. In the **Invite by email** section, enter the **Email address**.
3. Choose the **Role**: viewers may only read, editors make changes, admins also manage members.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

You always see the same confirmation, whether the address is known or not. If the person is already a member, nothing is sent.

## Manage open invitations

Under **Open invitations** you see all invitations that have not been answered yet, with the status **Open until** (date) or **Expired**. Invited people are not members yet.

- An invitation is valid for **7 days**.
- **Send again** sends a new e-mail and extends the deadline by 7 days. The old link stops working. After sending, there is a short wait of about a minute before you can resend the same invitation.
- **Revoke** withdraws the invitation. The link in the e-mail stops working. You can invite the person again at any time.

:::tip
Accepted and declined invitations disappear from the list.
:::

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the link in the invitation e-mail.
2. Sign in or create an account with **the same e-mail address** the invitation was sent to.
3. Click **Accept** or **Decline**.

If you already have an account, you can also find open invitations in the club picker under **Invitations** and answer them there.

Good to know:

- Your e-mail address must be confirmed. An unconfirmed address does not see any invitations.
- Nothing is accepted automatically. Even after signing up, you have to click **Accept** yourself.
- **Decline** changes nothing about your account.

## If the invitation does not work

- **Invalid or meant for another account:** The link belongs to a different e-mail address, was revoked or has already been answered.
- **Expired:** Ask a club admin to resend the invitation.
- **Club full:** The club has reached its maximum number of members. Ask an admin for help.

## What if the address has no account yet?

The invitation is sent anyway. The person can create an account via the link, confirm their e-mail address and then accept the invitation. If registration is restricted to invited people, an open invitation is the only way to create an account.
