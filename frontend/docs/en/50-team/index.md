# Team

A club consists of its members. What each person may do depends on their role (see [Roles](help:getting-started/roles)). New members join only through an invitation that they accept themselves.

- [Members](help:team/members): view the list, change roles, remove members, leave the club.
- [Invitations](help:team/invitations): invite people by e-mail and accept an invitation.
