# Members

Here you see who belongs to your club and manage their roles.

## View the member list

1. Open **Manage club**.
2. Switch to the **Members** tab.

The list shows name, e-mail, role and action. You can spot yourself by the **You** label. In large clubs the list is split into pages (10 entries each): use **Previous** and **Next** to browse.

Use the search field to find people by name or e-mail, and **Filter by role** to show only one role.

## Change a role

:::note
You need the Admin role for this.
:::

1. In the **Members** tab, open the role selector in the person's row.
2. Choose **Member**, **Viewer**, **Editor** or **Admin**.

A confirmation appears once the role is saved. What the roles mean is explained under [Roles](help:getting-started/roles).

## Remove a member

:::note
You need the Admin role for this.
:::

1. Click **Remove** in the person's row.
2. Confirm with **Remove member**.

The person can no longer see the club, its calendars or feeds. Their account remains. They can only return through a new [invitation](help:team/invitations).

## Leave the club

Every member can leave the club on their own.

1. Click **Leave club** in your own row.
2. Confirm with **Leave club**.

You lose access to all calendars, events and feeds. The other members keep it. As a member you do not see this list: use **Leave club** on [My club](help:getting-started/my-club).

## When it does not work

:::warning
A club always needs at least one member and, as long as there are other members, at least one admin.
:::

- **You are the only admin:** First make another member an admin. Only then can you change your role or leave the club.
- **You are the only member:** You cannot leave the club. Invite someone first or delete the club.
