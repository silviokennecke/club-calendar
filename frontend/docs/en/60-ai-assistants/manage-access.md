# Manage access

You find all AI apps you allowed access in your profile.

## See connected apps

1. Open your **Profile**.
2. Switch to the tab **Calendar & apps** and find the section **Connected apps**.

For each app you see its name (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or "Unknown app" with its redirect address; a self-declared name appears only as "Calls itself ..." below it) and whether it may **Read only** or **Read and drafts**, when it was connected and when it was last used. An empty list means you have not connected any app yet.

## Disconnect an app

1. Click **Disconnect** next to the app.
2. Confirm in the dialog with **Disconnect**.

The app loses access immediately. Events and drafts it created are kept. To use it again, connect it anew.

## Disconnected automatically

All connected apps automatically lose access when you

- sign out **everywhere**,
- change or reset your password,
- change your e-mail address or a passkey is deleted.

After that you need to connect the apps again.

## Limit

You can connect at most 20 apps at the same time. Disconnect apps you no longer need to make room.

:::tip
Disconnect apps you no longer use. This keeps the list tidy.
:::
