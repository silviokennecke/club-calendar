# AI assistants

You can connect an AI assistant (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) to the club calendar. Then you can ask it in a chat: "What is coming up next month?" or "Create a draft event for the summer party."

The connection is yours: the assistant acts on your behalf and may only do what you are allowed to do in your club. For every connection you decide whether it may only read or also create drafts, and you can end access at any time.

:::note
New events created by an assistant always stay drafts. Publishing and deleting events stays with you.
:::
