# Creating an account

You work in the club calendar with your own account. Signing in and signing up use the same page.

## Sign up

1. Open the sign-in page and enter your e-mail address. Click **Continue**.
2. If there is no account for that address yet, the sign-up form opens. Enter your **Name** and a **Password** (at least 8 characters), then repeat it under **Repeat password**.
3. Tick both boxes: you confirm that you have read and accept the privacy policy and the terms of use. You cannot continue without both. Click the document name to read it.
4. Click **Create account**.

![Sign-in page with email field, Continue and passkey option](shot:sign-up-email-step)

If the address already has an account, the page asks for your password instead, so you cannot create a duplicate.

## With Google, Microsoft or a passkey

Below **or** on the first step you find more ways: **Sign in with Google**, **Sign in with Microsoft**, **Sign in with Apple** (if enabled) and **Sign in with passkey**. A passkey signs you in with a fingerprint, face recognition or your device PIN. It works once you have set it up in your [profile](help:settings/passkeys).

If two-step sign-in (MFA) is on for your account, signing in with your password also asks for a second step. More under [Two-step sign-in (MFA)](help:settings/mfa).

## Confirm your e-mail address

After signing up you are signed in right away and see the **Confirm your email address** page. We send a 6-digit code to your address.

1. Open the e-mail and enter the code under **Confirmation code**.
2. You then continue straight into the app.

Until the address is confirmed, the rest of the app is locked: any other page sends you back to this page. You can only enter the code while signed in. Use **Sign out** on the page to leave it at any time.

The same applies if you sign in with the right password of an account that is not yet confirmed: you are signed in and land on this page. If no code is waiting, we send you a new one. Signing in with Microsoft also leads here, because Microsoft does not prove the address. With Google or Apple the code is skipped.

:::tip
Nothing arrived? Check your spam folder. **Send again** on the same page gets you a new code after a wait of a few minutes. The code is valid for 15 minutes. After five wrong entries you need a new one.
:::

## Forgot your password?

On the password step click **Forgot password?**. You get a link to set a new password. More under [Password](help:settings/password).

## Signing up with an invitation

If someone invited you to a club by e-mail, open the link in the mail. A notice at the top shows who invited you. Sign in or create an account, ideally with the address the invitation was sent to. Then confirm the invitation with **Accept** or dismiss it with **Decline**. An invitation is valid for 7 days.

:::note
You can only accept the invitation with the confirmed e-mail address it was sent to.
:::

:::warning
Some installations only allow new accounts with an invitation. You then see "Registration is only possible with an invitation". Ask your club's admins to invite you.
:::
