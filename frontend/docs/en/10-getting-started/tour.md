# Getting to know the app

This is how you find your way around. On a computer you have a sidebar on the left, on a smartphone a bar at the bottom.

![Sidebar with club and calendar switcher, sections, profile and help](shot:tour-sidebar)

## Switching club and calendar

At the top you see **Club** and **Calendar**. Each opens a menu on click:

- **Switch club**: go to another club, **Show all clubs** or **Create new club**.
- **Switch calendar**: go to another calendar of your club or to **Manage calendars**. Archived calendars are marked and read-only.

On a smartphone both are in one switcher in the top bar.

## The sidebar areas

- **Events**: **Calendar**, **All events**, **Templates** and **Import events**.
- **Set up calendar**: the calendar **Settings** and **Publish**.
- **Club**: **Manage club** (members, calendars) and **Places and audiences**.

At the bottom you find **My profile** (your name), **Help** and **Sign out**.

:::note
Viewers do not see **Import events** and **Publish**. More under [Roles in a club](help:getting-started/roles).
:::

## On a smartphone

At the bottom is a tab bar with **Calendar**, **Events**, **Publish** and **More**. Under **More** you find all other areas, switching clubs, the **Language** and **Sign out**. Your profile is behind your initials at the top right.

![Phone view with top bar and bottom tab bar](shot:tour-mobile-tabbar)

## Language and appearance

You set the **Language** (German or English) and the **Appearance** (**Light**, **Dark** or **Same as system**) in your [profile](help:settings/preferences). The language applies to your account, the appearance only to the current browser.

## Help

**Help** in the sidebar opens this guide. Use the search at the top to find the right page quickly.
