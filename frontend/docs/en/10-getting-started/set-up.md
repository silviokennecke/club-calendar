# Setting up a club and calendar

If you are not in a club yet, the setup takes you to your first calendar in two steps. You automatically become an admin of your club.

:::note
If someone invited you, you do not need this: accept the invitation and you already belong to a club. More under [Creating an account](help:getting-started/sign-up).
:::

## Step 1: Club

1. Enter the name under **Name of your club**, for example "Waldblick Shooting Club". All members see this name.
2. Click **Create club**.

![Setup, step 1: enter the club name](shot:set-up-club-step)

## Step 2: Calendar

1. A suggestion is filled in as **Name of the calendar** (the current year is part of it). Change it if you like.
2. The time zone is detected from your browser. You can change it later in the settings.
3. Click **Create calendar**.

You then land in the empty calendar and can add your [first event](help:events/first-event).

:::tip
**Back** lets you correct the club name while you are still in this setup. **Skip for now** postpones the calendar. You create more calendars under **Manage club** on the **Calendars** tab.
:::

## More clubs

You can be a member of several clubs. Choose **Create new club** in the sidebar and the setup starts again.
