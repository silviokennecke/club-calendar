# My club

**My club** is your personal page in a club. You find it in the navigation. Members see only this page. For viewers, editors and admins it is an addition to the club calendar. It has two tabs.

## Calendar

Shows the events of the feeds you chose, as a month or a list. If the club uses audiences, you can filter by audience; your choice from the subscription is preselected. Changing it here does not change your subscription. Click an event to see its details.

![The Calendar tab in My club with the audience filter](shot:my-club-calendar)

## Subscription

Here you decide what appears in your calendar.

1. Under **Feeds**, tick the feeds you want. Next to each feed you see why it is available to you, for example "For all members" or "Assigned to you".
2. Under **Audiences**, optionally choose audiences. Without a selection you get the events of all audiences.
3. Click **Save selection**.

![The Subscription tab with feeds, audiences and personal link](shot:my-club-subscription)

Below you find **Your personal link**. Add it to your calendar app, the steps are under [Subscribe to a calendar](help:publishing/subscribe). The link always contains your saved selection.

:::warning
The personal link is only for you. It can also contain private feeds. Do not pass it on.
:::

If the link fell into the wrong hands, renew it in your profile, see [Profile and name](help:settings/profile). Afterwards enter the new link in your calendar app.

## Leave the club

Click **Leave club** at the top of the page and confirm. You then lose access to the club and your subscription for it is deleted. You can come back through an [invitation](help:team/invitations) or by opening a feed of the club again, if the club allows that.
