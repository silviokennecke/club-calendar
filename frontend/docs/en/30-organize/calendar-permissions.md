# Calendar permissions

:::note
Only club admins can give permissions.
:::

Regular members do not see the club calendar. With a calendar permission, a member may edit **one** specific calendar without becoming an editor for the whole club. This suits, for example, the person who looks after the youth group's calendar.

## Where do I find them?

Open the calendar's **Settings** and choose the **Permissions** tab.

![The Permissions tab with two people](shot:calendar-permissions)

## Give a permission

1. Under **Add a club member**, search by name or e-mail address. All members of the club are offered, with e-mail address and role.
2. Choose the person and click **Add**.

## Remove a permission

In the list, click **Remove** next to the person and confirm. The person stays in the club but can no longer open the calendar.

## What the person may do

In this calendar they may:

- create, change and delete events, event series and templates, and use import, export and bulk actions
- manage [badges](help:organize/properties) and [extras](help:organize/features)
- only view [holidays](help:organize/holidays) and the calendar's settings

They cannot create new locations or audiences: only the existing ones are offered. In an import, only known locations are possible. Everything else in the club, such as feeds, members and other calendars, stays closed to them.

When someone leaves the club or is removed, their permissions end too.
