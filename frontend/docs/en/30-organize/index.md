# Organize

Create locations, badges, extras, audiences and templates once, then just pick them whenever you add an event. This makes entering events faster and more consistent.

## Recommended order

1. [Locations](help:organize/locations): where your events take place.
2. [Audiences](help:organize/target-groups): who they are meant for.
3. [Badges](help:organize/properties) and [Extras](help:organize/features): badges and optional additions.
4. [Templates](help:organize/templates): bundle time, location and more for recurring events.
5. After that: [Series](help:organize/series), [Holidays](help:organize/holidays), [Bulk actions](help:organize/bulk), [Import](help:organize/import) and [Export](help:organize/export).

:::note
Locations and audiences belong to the whole club. Badges, extras and templates belong to a single calendar.
:::
