# Locations

:::note
Editors and admins can create, change and delete locations. Viewers can only see them.
:::

A location is a meeting place of your club, e.g. the clubhouse, with an address and optionally a position on the map. Locations belong to the whole club and are available in all of its calendars.

## Where do I find them?

In the sidebar under **Club**, open **Places and audiences**, then the **Locations** tab. An overview map shows all locations that have a position.

## Create a location

1. Choose **Create place**.
2. Enter the **Name** and start typing in the **Street** field (from 2 characters). Pick a suggestion: **Street**, **ZIP**, **City**, **Country** and the map position are filled in, the name stays as it is. You can also enter all fields by hand.
3. Optionally set the position: click the map or drag the marker. Without the map, use **Enter coordinates by hand** (latitude and longitude).
4. Choose **Save place**.

At least a name or a street is required.

:::tip
If you leave the **Street** field while **ZIP**, **City** and **Country** are still empty, they are filled in automatically when the address is unambiguous. Values you already entered are never overwritten.
:::

Only addresses are searched, not points of interest or venues. For a clubhouse, enter the name yourself and search for the street.

![The "Create new place" form with street suggestions and map](shot:locations-form)

## Edit and delete

Choose the location in the list to edit it. To delete it, use **Delete place**. The dialog first shows what is affected: events at this place have no place afterwards, templates lose their default place. The events themselves stay.

## Use it in an event

Choose the **Location** field in the event. With **Create new location** you can add a new one right there. If the location has a position, you see it on the map in the event. You can also store a location as the **Default place** in a [template](help:organize/templates).
