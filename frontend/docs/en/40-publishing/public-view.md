# The public view

Every feed has a web page that works without signing in. It shows the club and feed name and all published events. All times are in the club's time zone.

## What visitors see

- A **Month** or **List** view of the events.
- Events of the selected calendars and the holidays.
- A search with suggestions (up to 10 results; **Show all results** opens the list with every match) and, if the club uses audiences, a filter by audience. When subscribing, the chosen audience is carried over.
- Visitors never see drafts.

![Public feed page in month view with events and holidays](shot:public-view-month)

## Open an event

Clicking an event shows date, time, place with a map, badges, extras, audiences and the note. With **Add to calendar** visitors download an ICS file for this event only.

## Subscribe to the calendar

With **Subscribe to calendar** visitors add the whole calendar to their app. The steps are in [Subscribe to a calendar](help:publishing/subscribe).

## If you are signed in

When you are signed in, a slim bar at the top shows **Signed in as** and your name, with links to your account and **Sign out**.

In the **Subscribe to calendar** menu you then also find **Add to my account**, or **Show in my account** if you already added it. Your account is the easiest way to keep the calendar: you find the events under [My club](help:getting-started/my-club). If you are not a member of the club yet, you first confirm that you want to join it. The club decides whether this is possible; otherwise ask for an [invitation](help:team/invitations).

## If you are signed in

When you are signed in, a slim bar at the top shows **Signed in as** and your name, with links **Go to my account** and **Sign out**.

In the **Subscribe to calendar** menu you then also find **Add to my account**, or **Show in my account** if you already added it. Your account is the easiest way to keep the calendar: you find the events under [My club](help:getting-started/my-club). If you are not a member of the club yet, you first confirm that you want to join it. The club decides whether this is possible; otherwise ask for an [invitation](help:team/invitations).

## If nothing shows up

- **No events published yet:** the club has not published anything yet. Subscribing already works.
- **This calendar is private:** the link is incomplete or no longer valid, e.g. after a new secret. Ask the club for the current link.
- **Calendar not found:** the feed does not exist or was deleted, or all selected calendars are archived.
