When you subscribe to the calendar, all events of the club appear in your own calendar app. New and changed events show up by themselves, so you do not have to do anything. You cannot break anything: you can delete the subscription at any time.

You find the calendar link on the club's calendar page: tap **Subscribe to calendar** and then **Copy link**. Then continue here.

## Which app do you use?

:::tabs key=app platform

:::tab ios iPhone / iPad

On an iPhone or iPad a single tap often does it: on the calendar page tap **Subscribe to calendar**. The Calendar app opens and asks whether you want to add the subscription. If that does not happen, these steps help. You need the copied calendar link.

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Apps** and then **Calendar**.
3. Tap **Calendar Accounts** and then **Add Account**.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-ios-3 | Calendar Accounts page with the Add Account row -->
4. Tap **Other** at the bottom and then **Add Subscribed Calendar**.
5. Tap the **Server** field, hold your finger on it for a moment and tap **Paste**. Your calendar link now appears there.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-ios-5 | Server field with the pasted calendar link -->
6. Tap **Next** at the top right.
7. Tap **Save** at the top right.

Done! Open the **Calendar** app. The club's events are there now.

**Not working?** The **Server** field stays empty? Then the link was not copied yet. Go back to the calendar page and tap **Copy link**. Events sometimes appear only after a few minutes. That is normal.

:::

:::tab macos Mac

You need the copied calendar link and the **Calendar** app on your Mac.

1. Open the **Calendar** app.
2. Click **File** in the menu bar at the top and then **New Calendar Subscription…**.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-macos-2 | File menu with New Calendar Subscription -->
3. Paste the calendar link (press **Cmd** + **V**) and click **Subscribe**.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-macos-3 | Subscribe window with the pasted link -->
4. Give the calendar a name and pick a color. This is just for you.
5. Under **Auto-refresh** choose for example **Every 15 minutes** or **Every hour**.
6. Click **OK**.

Done! The calendar is now in the list on the left. A tick means its events are shown.

**Not working?** **New Calendar Subscription…** is greyed out? Click into the Calendar window first. If you also have an iPhone, you often see the calendar there after a short while too.

:::

:::tab outlook Outlook

These steps are for Outlook on the web and the new Outlook. If you use classic Outlook on your computer, see the end of this guide. You need the copied calendar link.

1. Open Outlook and switch to the **Calendar**.
2. Click **Add calendar** on the left.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-outlook-2 | Add calendar button in the left bar -->
3. Click **Subscribe from web**.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-outlook-3 | Subscribe from web option -->
4. Paste the calendar link into the field (press **Ctrl** + **V**).
5. Give the calendar a name you will recognize.
6. Click **Import**.

Done! The calendar appears in your list on the left. Classic Outlook on your computer: click **File**, then **Account Settings** and **Calendar subscription** (in some versions it is called **Internet Calendars**). Paste the calendar link there and confirm with **Add**.

**Not working?** You do not see the events right away? That is normal: Outlook sometimes needs a few hours until changes appear. Check that the tick next to the calendar on the left is set.

:::

:::tab google Google Calendar

**Important:** Subscribing only works on the website on a computer, not in the Android app. Afterwards you also see the calendar in the app on your phone. You need the copied calendar link.

1. On a computer open **calendar.google.com** and sign in.
2. On the left click the plus (**+**) next to **Other calendars**.
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-google-2 | Plus next to Other calendars -->
3. Click **From URL**.
4. Paste the calendar link into the **URL of calendar** field (press **Ctrl** + **V**).
   <!-- SHOT: subscribe-google-4 | URL of calendar field with the pasted link -->
5. Click **Add calendar**.

Done! The calendar is on the left under **Other calendars**. Google does not show new events right away. It can take a few hours until changes appear. That is normal.

**Not working?** The calendar is missing on your phone? Open the Google Calendar app, tap the menu and then **Settings**. Tap the club's calendar and turn on **Sync**.

## Problems that occur with every app

The link does not work or the app shows an error? Copy the link from the calendar page again and make sure it was pasted completely. For a private calendar the secret part at the end belongs to the link. If nothing shows up still, contact the club that runs the calendar.
