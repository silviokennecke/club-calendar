# Search and filter

Search finds an event without paging through the months. The target group filter hides events that do not concern you.

## Search in the calendar

1. Click the **Search events** field above the calendar.
2. Type at least two letters. The search covers the whole calendar, not just the month shown, and looks in name, note, location and features.
3. Choose a result from the list, with the mouse or with the arrow keys and Enter. The event opens, and the calendar behind it jumps to its date.

Each result shows date, time and location; drafts are marked. With more than 20 results, the list says “Showing 20 of … matches. Refine your search.” Press Esc to close the list.

![Calendar with a search term and the result list](shot:search-filter-calendar-search)

## Filter by target group

If your club has created [target groups](help:organize/target-groups), you see one button per target group next to the search.

- Click one or more target groups: the calendar shows only events that belong to at least one of them.
- Click again to deselect a target group.
- **Reset filters** clears everything.

While a filter is active, the calendar hides holidays.

## In the event list

The [event list](help:events/event-list) has its own search field (**Search events…**, name only), the same target group filter, and tabs for the status **All**, **Published** and **Draft**.

:::note
There is no filter for properties or features. You find features through the calendar search; in the list they have their own column.
:::
