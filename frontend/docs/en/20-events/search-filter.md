# Search and filter

Search finds an event without paging through the months. The audience filter hides events that do not concern you.

## Search in the calendar

1. Click the **Search events** field above the calendar.
2. Type at least two letters. The search covers the whole calendar, not just the month shown, and looks in name, note, location and extras.
3. Choose a result from the list, with the mouse or with the arrow keys and Enter. The event opens, and the calendar behind it jumps to its date.

The list shows at most 10 results with date, time and location; drafts are marked. If there are more, it says “Showing 10 of … matches.” The last entry, **Show all results**, is always there: it opens the [event list](help:events/event-list) with your search term. Press Esc to close the list.

![Calendar with a search term and the result list](shot:search-filter-calendar-search)

## Filter by audience

If your club has created [audiences](help:organize/target-groups), you see one button per audience next to the search.

- Click one or more audiences: the calendar shows only events that belong to at least one of them.
- Click again to deselect an audience.
- **Reset filters** clears everything.

While a filter is active, the calendar hides holidays.

## In the event list

The [event list](help:events/event-list) has its own search field (**Search events…**, name only; **Show all results** leads there too), the same audience filter, and tabs for the status **All**, **Published** and **Draft**.

:::note
There is no filter for badges or extras. You find extras through the calendar search; in the list they have their own column.
:::
