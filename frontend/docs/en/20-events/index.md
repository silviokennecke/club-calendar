Events are the heart of the club calendar. Here you learn how to create, change and find them again.

- [Your first event](help:events/first-event): create an event and understand every field
- [Edit, move and delete events](help:events/edit)
- [Views: month, week and list](help:events/views)
- [Search and filter](help:events/search-filter)

Recurring events are created as an [event series](help:organize/series); change several events at once with [bulk actions](help:organize/bulk).
