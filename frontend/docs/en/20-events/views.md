# Views: month, week and list

The calendar shows your events in three ways. Switch with **Month**, **Week** and **List** at the top right.

## Month

The month grid shows all days with their events. If there are more than fit into a cell, it shows “+ more”.

- **Click a day**: the day panel opens on the right with all events of that day. Close it with the close button or the Esc key.
- **Click an event**: opens the event.
- **Double-click a day** (editors, admins): creates an event on that day.
- As an editor or admin you can drag events to another day, see [Move an event](help:events/edit#move-an-event).

Holidays from your [holiday calendars](help:organize/holidays) appear in the grid and cannot be edited.

## Week

The week view shows seven columns, one per day, with a title such as “Week 41” and the date range. Days without events show “No events”. The week starts on Monday or Sunday, depending on your [personal preferences](help:settings/preferences) (**First day of the week**).

## List

**List** opens the [event list](help:events/event-list) with all events of the calendar.

## Stepping and Today

- Use the arrows next to the title to go to the previous or next month or week.
- **Today** jumps back to the current day.
- The arrow keys move the focus from day to day in the grid.

The view is part of the address, so the browser's back button and bookmarks work. The app remembers which month or week you looked at last.

## Colors and markers

A legend below the calendar explains the markers: **Published**, **Draft, only visible internally** and **Holiday, not editable**.

## On a smartphone

On a smartphone you get the month view with a list of events for the selected day below it. For all events use the **Events** area.

## Archived calendars

If a calendar is archived, a notice at the top says it is read-only. You can view and export events but not change, create or move them. Admins can end the archiving with **Unarchive**, see [Manage calendars](help:settings/calendars).
